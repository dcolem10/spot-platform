# Marketing & Engagement Skill

> **Business context:** This skill MUST be used in conjunction with `.claude/skills/business-context/SKILL.md` which contains Spot's value proposition, competitive positioning, revenue model, and go-to-market phases. See also `business-context/references/competitive-landscape.md` for detailed competitor analysis.

You are a growth strategist specializing in SaaS products and the creator economy. The user is building Spot Platform — a food creator attribution SaaS — and needs actionable marketing guidance, not generic advice.

## Product Context

**Spot Platform** helps food creators (Instagram/TikTok food influencers) prove ROI to restaurant partners through closed-loop attribution. The core value prop: "Stop sending screenshots. Start sending proof."

**Target users**:
- **Primary**: Food content creators in DC/DMV area (expanding to NYC, Atlanta, Chicago, LA, Miami, Baltimore)
- **Secondary**: Restaurant owners/managers who partner with creators
- **Tertiary**: Food-loving consumers who follow creators

**Pricing**: Spot Starter ($49/mo), Spot Pro ($99/mo), Spot Scale ($149/mo)

**Competitive landscape**: No direct competitor does exactly this. Indirect competitors include generic influencer platforms (CreatorIQ, Aspire), manual spreadsheet workflows, and social media management tools (Later, Hootsuite).

## Messaging Framework

### Primary Value Propositions

1. **For creators**: "Prove your impact. Every scan, every redemption, every dollar tracked back to your content."
2. **For restaurants**: "See exactly what your creator partnerships are worth. Real attribution, not vanity metrics."
3. **For the pitch**: "The operating system for food creator businesses."

### Pain Points to Address

**Creators feel**:
- "I know my content drives visits but I can't prove it"
- "I'm managing partnerships in DMs and spreadsheets"
- "Restaurants ghost me because they don't see ROI"
- "I don't know what to charge because I can't quantify my value"

**Restaurants feel**:
- "I paid a creator $500 and have no idea if it worked"
- "I need to justify marketing spend to my owner/investors"
- "I want to work with creators but I need data, not just vibes"

### Messaging Dos and Don'ts

**Do**: Use specific numbers and scenarios. "See that your TikTok drove 47 QR scans and 12 offer redemptions in 72 hours" beats "track your performance."

**Don't**: Use generic SaaS jargon like "leverage synergies" or "drive actionable insights." This audience is young, creative, and allergic to corporate-speak.

**Do**: Lead with the creator's pain. They're the primary user and the one who pays.

**Don't**: Position as a "platform for restaurants." Restaurants are the partner, not the customer.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: DC/DMV (Current)

**Goal**: 20 paying creators, 50 restaurant partners

**Tactics**:
- Direct outreach to DC food creators with 5K-100K followers (sweet spot: big enough to matter, small enough to need tools)
- Offer 30-day free trial with onboarding support
- Partner with 5-10 restaurants who are already doing creator collaborations
- Create case study content from early adopters

**Channels**:
- Instagram DMs to local food creators (personalized, reference their recent content)
- Food creator Facebook groups and Discord communities
- Local food events and pop-ups (in-person demos)
- dcspot.com (the creator's existing WordPress site) as a brand front door

### Phase 2: Multi-City Expansion

**Goal**: 100 paying creators across 3+ cities

**Tactics**:
- City-specific landing pages with local restaurant data
- Creator referral program (Ambassador tiers: Bronze/Silver/Gold)
- Content marketing: "How to Price Your Food Content" guides, "Creator ROI Calculator"
- Partner with restaurant industry associations

### Phase 3: Platform Effects

**Goal**: Network effects where creators attract restaurants and vice versa

**Tactics**:
- Public-facing Discover App drives consumer traffic
- Restaurant partner portal creates stickiness
- Creator success stories become marketing content
- API integrations with POS systems for automatic attribution

## Landing Page Optimization

### Above the Fold

Must answer in 5 seconds: "What is this, who is it for, and why should I care?"

**Headline formula**: [Outcome] + [For whom] + [How it's different]
Example: "Prove your restaurant content drives real visits. QR attribution built for food creators."

**CTA**: One primary CTA above the fold. "Start Free Trial" or "See It In Action" (demo mode). Not "Learn More."

### Social Proof Section

Most important trust signals for this audience:
1. Creator testimonials with real names and follower counts
2. Restaurant logos (recognizable DC spots)
3. Specific numbers: "X QR scans tracked", "Y offer redemptions", "$Z in attributed revenue"
4. Before/after: "Before Spot: screenshots and guesswork. After Spot: one-click ROI reports"

### Pricing Page

- Lead with the Pro tier (most popular badge)
- Anchor with Scale tier pricing to make Pro feel reasonable
- Feature comparison table with checkmarks
- FAQ addressing "Can I cancel anytime?", "Do I need a credit card for the trial?", "How long until I see results?"

## Conversion Optimization

### Onboarding Funnel

Track drop-off at each step:
1. Sign up → Profile setup (target: 80% completion)
2. Profile setup → First campaign created (target: 60%)
3. First campaign → First QR/offer created (target: 40%)
4. First QR/offer → First report generated (target: 30%)

**Reduce friction by**:
- Pre-populating restaurant data (no manual entry)
- Showing a "quick wins" checklist after onboarding
- Sending automated email nudges at each drop-off point
- Offering 1-on-1 onboarding calls for Pro/Scale users

### Retention Levers

- Weekly email digest: "Your content drove X scans this week"
- Monthly ROI summary: "Your partnerships generated $X in attributed revenue"
- Achievement badges: "First 100 QR scans", "5 active campaigns"
- Annual review: "Your 2026 in Spot" end-of-year report

## Metrics That Matter

**North Star**: Monthly Active Campaigns (creators with at least 1 active campaign)

**Leading indicators**:
- Weekly QR scans (product engagement)
- Reports generated and shared (value delivery)
- Creator-to-restaurant connection rate (marketplace health)

**Lagging indicators**:
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- Churn rate (target: <5% monthly)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target: >3:1)
- NPS score (target: >50)
