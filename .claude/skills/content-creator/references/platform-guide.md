## 1. Instagram

### Reels (Primary Format)
- **Ideal length:** 30-60 seconds (algorithm sweet spot)
- **Hook window:** First 1-2 seconds determine if someone stops scrolling
- **Best hooks for food:** Close-up of cheese pull, sizzling pan, plating in motion, "I found the best [dish] in [city]"
- **Music:** Trending audio boosts reach significantly. Creators should use Instagram's trending audio library
- **Captions:** On-screen text captions are essential — 85% of video is watched without sound
- **Cover image:** Choose a frame that looks good as a static thumbnail in the grid
- **Call to action:** "Save this for your next visit" performs better than "like and follow"

### Carousels
- **Ideal slides:** 5-10 images
- **First slide:** Must stop the scroll — the "hero dish" or a text hook like "5 dishes you HAVE to try at [Restaurant]"
- **Last slide:** Call to action or save prompt
- **Mix:** Alternate between wide shots (ambiance), close-ups (food), and detail shots (textures, ingredients)
- **Text overlays:** Brief, readable — dish name + one-line descriptor per slide
- **High save rate:** Carousels get saved more than any other format, which boosts algorithm ranking

### Stories
- **Frame count:** 3-8 frames per story set is optimal. More than 10 and drop-off increases sharply
- **Style:** Casual, authentic, slightly unpolished. Stories that look like ads get skipped
- **Stickers:** Polls ("Would you try this?"), question boxes ("Ask me anything about this spot"), and location stickers all boost engagement
- **Swipe-up / Link sticker:** Use for reservation links or promo codes
- **Timing:** Post stories during or immediately after the restaurant visit for maximum authenticity

## 2. TikTok

### Video Format
- **Ideal length:** 15-45 seconds (shorter performs better for discovery; longer for retention)
- **Vertical only:** 9:16 aspect ratio, no exceptions
- **Hook:** Must capture attention in the first 0.5-1 second. Food TikTok hooks: dramatic reveal, overhead pour shot, extreme close-up, "POV: you just found the best [dish] in [city]"
- **Trends:** TikTok is trend-driven more than any other platform. Creators should adapt trending sounds, formats, and memes to their restaurant content
- **Text-to-speech:** Popular for narration, adds accessibility
- **Comments:** TikTok's algorithm weighs comments heavily. Content that sparks debate or questions performs better ("Is this worth $45?")

### TikTok-Specific Considerations
- **Younger demographic:** Skews 18-34, more price-sensitive audience
- **Discovery-first:** TikTok's algorithm shows content to non-followers more aggressively than Instagram
- **Local SEO:** TikTok is increasingly used as a search engine for "best restaurants in [city]". Content should include location names naturally
- **Duet/Stitch:** Creators can respond to restaurant's own TikToks or other food content, creating conversational content

## 3. YouTube Shorts

### Format
- **Max 60 seconds**, but 30-45 seconds is the sweet spot
- **Vertical (9:16)** like TikTok
- **Can be repurposed** from TikTok/Reels but performs better when native
- **Title matters:** YouTube Shorts display a title — make it searchable ("Best Thai Food in DC")
- **Monetization:** YouTube Shorts Fund pays creators directly, which is a bonus incentive

## 4. Cross-Posting Strategy

Most food creators post across multiple platforms. A typical workflow:

1. **Film once** at the restaurant (30-60 min of raw footage)
2. **Edit a primary version** (usually Reels or TikTok, depending on creator's strongest platform)
3. **Adapt for other platforms** — same core footage, different editing for each platform's norms
4. **Stories are separate** — filmed more casually during the visit

When defining campaign deliverables, understand that asking for content on 2-3 platforms isn't 2-3x the work — it's more like 1.5x since the filming is shared. Price accordingly.

## 5. Optimal Posting Times (Food Niche, US Eastern)

| Day | Best Times | Why |
|-----|-----------|-----|
| Monday-Thursday | 11:30am-1pm, 5-7pm | Lunch and dinner decision windows |
| Friday | 11am-1pm, 4-6pm | Weekend planning starts |
| Saturday | 10am-12pm, 5-7pm | Brunch and dinner inspiration |
| Sunday | 10am-1pm, 4-6pm | Brunch crowd + week-ahead planning |

These are guidelines. Creators should check their own analytics for their audience's active times.

## 6. Hashtag Strategy (Food Content)

### Tier 1: High-Volume (discovery)
- #foodtiktok #foodie #foodreview #eeeeeats #foodporn
- Use 1-2 per post max

### Tier 2: Mid-Volume (niche)
- #[city]food #[city]eats #[cuisine]food #[neighborhood]eats
- Use 2-3 per post — these drive local discovery

### Tier 3: Low-Volume (targeted)
- #[restaurantname] #[specificdish] #[localfoodscene]
- Use 1-2 per post — builds searchability for the restaurant

### Tier 4: Campaign-Specific
- Restaurant may request a branded hashtag (e.g., #SpotPartner, #[RestaurantName]Eats)
- Keep to 1 max — too many branded tags feel like ads

**Total hashtags per post:** 5-8 is optimal for food content on Instagram. TikTok uses fewer (3-5) and relies more on caption keywords for SEO.
