# Spot Value Proposition — Deep Reference

## The Problem (In the Founder's Words)

When a DC restaurant partners with a food creator, the entire process runs on guesswork:
- Restaurants spend $500–$2,000 per creator partnership with zero data on whether it drove customers
- Creators charge based on follower count because they can't prove their posts drive real visits
- Audiences have no centralized place to find creator-recommended restaurants and deals

Every other advertising channel has solved attribution — Google Ads shows cost-per-click, Instagram shows impressions, DoorDash shows orders. But creator-to-restaurant partnerships? Total black box.

## The Solution: Closed-Loop Attribution

1. Creator discovers restaurant, proposes campaign
2. Restaurant approves partnership and content
3. Creator posts with unique QR code, promo code, or tracking link
4. Audience scans/redeems at restaurant
5. Spot tracks every visit through POS integration (Square, Clover, Toast)
6. Both sides see real attribution data — which creator drove which customers

## Creator Acquisition Strategy

1. Darren's existing DC Spot network (warm outreach)
2. Ambassador program — existing creators earn rewards for referrals (bronze/silver/gold tiers)
3. Content marketing — publish attribution case studies showing real ROI data
4. Social proof — "My content drove 200 customers to 8 restaurants last month" > any media kit
5. Free tier consideration — may introduce limited free tier to reduce signup friction

## Why Creators Choose Spot Over Alternatives

- **vs. Mustard:** Spot is creator-controlled (not restaurant-controlled), 6x cheaper, focuses on attribution data
- **vs. Spreadsheets:** Professional tools replace manual tracking, portfolio building, actual ROI proof
- **vs. Agency model:** Creator keeps full creative control and client relationship
- **vs. "Free meals" partnerships:** Spot turns casual content creation into measurable, recurring income
